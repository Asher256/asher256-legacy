## Introduction

Mister Kid is a side-scrolling platform game that will transport you to a
colorful and thrilling adventure through 12 levels and 5 worlds. With each
level, you will encounter new obstacles, enemies, and challenges that will test
your skills and wits. From the forest to the world of sand, you will need to
keep your eyes peeled and stay focused if you want to succeed.

## Controls

Here is a quick rundown of the controls:
- Right and Left arrows: move the character.
- Alt: Jump.
- Enter: Pause the game.

## Play in recent computers

By using the DOS emulator DOSBOX (or DOSBOX-X), the game can be played on more
recent operating systems like GNU/Linux, Microsoft Windows and Mac OS X.

In order to ensure that the game runs at its optimal performance, it is highly
recommended to modify the "cycles" option under the "[cpu]" section in the
DOSBOX configuration file located at "~/.dosbox/dosbox*.conf":
```
[cpu]
cycles=max
```

## The ennemies

Mister Kid is full of different enemies that you will encounter in each
world. Here are the enemies you will face:

### World 1: The Forest
In the forest, you will encounter Goombas and birds that are relatively easy to
beat.

### World 2: The Frozen World
The frozen world is a bit more challenging. You will face penguins that follow
you and Tinky, the little round bird, that will attack you by surprise if you
are in front of it.

### World 3: The World of Pokemon
In the world of Pokemon, you will meet Pikachu, who is intelligent and waits
until you forget about it to chase you. Flying Jigglypuff will locate you and
fly towards you.

### World 4: The World of the Invincibles
The world of the invincibles is the most challenging world of all. The enemies
have no weakness, so you will need to run for your life!

### World 5: The World of Sand
In the final world, the world of sand, you will need to keep an eye on the time
as the watch is your enemy. Additionally, the snakes will team up and follow
you, so you will need to be careful.
